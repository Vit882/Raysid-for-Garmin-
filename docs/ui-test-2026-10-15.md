# In-depth UI test – build 82 (version 1.1), 15 Oct 2026

I went through the whole app as a tester in four areas: menu and settings, screens and buttons, mapping and upload, connection and alarms. For each item I went through combinations of states. The fixes were then checked once more by another independent pass, which found 4 bugs caused by the fixes themselves. Those are fixed too.

The code compiles with the strictest type checking. I tested the parser by simulation: delimiter 0x84 and 0x10, split packets, junk and 5000 notifications with random errors. It has not been tried on the watch or in the Garmin simulator.

## What changes in the controls
- **Debug screen:** with the debug screen enabled, UP and DOWN do nothing. Previously UP secretly started recording or switched the chart.
- **API key:** when it is already entered, the cursor starts on OK. So START closes the screen without change.
- **Only chosen Raysid:** cannot be turned on until a specific Raysid is chosen. "pick a Raysid first" is shown.
- **Raysid divider:** without a connection the list does not open; "Disconnected" is shown right away.
- **Default language:** on first start the language is chosen according to the watch.
- **Clock:** shows 12-hour time if the watch is set to it.
- **Alarm length:** "long" is now called "long (8×)". Rapid rise uses the same lengths as the other alarms (previously 1/1/3).

## Fixed bugs
**Alarms and signalling**
1. The red flashlight did not light up during an alarm with divider 1:1, with flashing turned off, or with clicking overload, i.e. exactly at high cps. It now flashes for the whole duration of the alarm.
2. The "clicking off" warning every 2 s and the clicks drowned out the alarm tone and vibration. Alarms now take priority.
3. The alarm kept running even after alarms were turned off in the menu and after the Raysid disconnected. It is now cut off by turning off any alarm and by disconnecting.
4. During overload (OVL), an alarm sounded only when the value exceeded the limit. Now during OVL every enabled dose or cps alarm sounds, as the help promises.
5. Rapid rise computed the background by number of readings. The Raysid sends data about every 0.25 s, so the "background over 20 s" was really 2.4 s, and a slower rise while walking towards a source did not trigger the alarm. It is now computed by time.
6. After restoring the connection with the Reconnect button or after an automatic restart, the alarm and OVL stayed lit.

**Bluetooth and Raysid**

7. One damaged packet stopped data reading for 5 to 40 s, with no values and no alarms. The parser now recovers right at the next packet.
8. After each connection with delimiter 0x84, nothing was displayed for about 10 notifications and then old values arrived. Data are now read from the first notification.
9. The automatic reconnect after 30 s could interrupt a pairing in progress.
10. The option "Several Raysids → strongest signal" was ignored when the chosen Raysid was in range. The option "any" switched back by itself after the next connection.
11. A delayed Raysid reply to an older settings read briefly reverted a toggle that had just been switched in the Raysid menu.
12. After connecting a different unit, the Raysid menu showed the settings of the previous one. It now shows "?" until the Raysid reports its state.
13. A ping could get into the middle of a settings read, so the Raysid did not reply.
14. One rejected write emptied the whole command queue including the ping.
15. When the Bluetooth profile registration failed, Reconnect did nothing.
16. After "Forget history", the "Only chosen" toggle stayed displayed as on. When choosing a different Raysid with the lock on, the watch did not reconnect.

**Mapping and upload**

17. Any 4xx web error (e.g. 404 during an outage or with a wrong address) deleted unsent chunks as "rejected". Now only batches that the server actually rejected because of their content (400, 409, 413, 422) are discarded. Others are sent again.
18. Delete unsent first sent the last pending chunk to the web and only then deleted.
19. After deleting, a late web response could delete a new, not yet sent chunk.
20. When full storage stopped recording, raising "When full" did not help; recording could not be turned on again.
21. After an app restart, it was forgotten that storage was full. The app then created a short route and ended it immediately, with autostart on every start.
22. The screen showed a red REC even when points were not being stored (Raysid disconnected or in OVL). It now shows "waiting for Raysid" or "OVL" in orange.
23. The first point after OVL included in its average the clipped values from the time of the overload.
24. After mapping was disabled, unsent points stopped being uploaded and this was not visible anywhere. They are now still uploaded and the menu shows "queued N".
25. The "key rejected" message stayed even after the key was fixed in Garmin Connect and after a successful upload.
26. Uploading could get stuck forever if the web did not respond. It is now retried after 2 minutes.
27. An app crash between two writes could leave a route unclosed on the web or mess up the point count. The point count is now recalculated at startup.
28. On app exit, a request was sent whose response could no longer arrive.

**Menu and screens**

29. Paused recording was not visible on the main screen and the Žhavá místa menu said "off". It now shows "paused".
30. Menu labels were not refreshed after returning from a submenu. This affected Žhavá místa (points after deleting), Web (verification result), Alarms and alarm groups. The main Alarms menu now shows "on" only when at least one alarm is enabled.
31. A custom dose limit was rounded in the menu, e.g. 9.99 was shown as "10". Cps limits were shown as "1.00 kcps", now as "1000 cps".
32. With a custom value set, the cursor in the list was on the first item instead of "Custom value...".
33. Long texts overflowed the round edge. This affected the status line with the Raysid name and error, the Web line on the Mapping screen and several menu items. Longer texts are now shortened or omit the name, and menu items are shorter, e.g. "Record on start", "Several Raysids" and "Test connection".
34. The arrows "→" and "…" in the help may show as squares on the watch. I replaced them with ">" and "...".
35. The chart without data showed "collecting data..." even when disconnected, and after pressing UP it was not visible that the length had changed.
36. After an outage, the chart did not stretch again to the data it has.
37. Flashing during an alarm could leave the debug screen red.
38. Values turned grey only after the next packet, even when data were not arriving.
39. A corrupted stored setting of a different type could crash the app already at startup.
40. Every UP wrote all 24 settings at once. It now writes only what changed.

## Knowingly not changed
- **BACK** still exits the app immediately, as stated in the help. The route is closed properly, so nothing is lost.
- **Default alarms on first install** (cps 1000 and rapid rise on, dose off) stay as they were.
- **Divider merging:** the watch remains the master. A divider change made directly in the Raysid app on the phone is overwritten by the watch after connecting.
- **Chart** during an outage connects the last point before the outage with the first one after it with a line (cosmetic).
