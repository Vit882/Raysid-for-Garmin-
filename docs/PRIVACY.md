# Privacy policy – Raysid for Garmin

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Raysid for Garmin is an unofficial, open-source Connect IQ app (GPL-3.0-or-later). It is not made by or affiliated with IN-NEW, the maker of the Raysid dosimeter.

## What the app processes

- **Dosimeter readings** (dose rate, counts per second, Raysid battery) received over Bluetooth from your Raysid dosimeter.
- **GPS position and time**, only while you record a mapping route.
- **Your Žhavá místa API key**, if you enter one in the app or in the app settings in Garmin Connect.

All of this is stored only on your watch.

## What leaves your watch

Nothing, unless you turn on upload to Žhavá místa. Then the recorded route points (position, time, dose rate, cps) are sent through the Garmin Connect app on your phone to [zhavamista.cz](https://www.zhavamista.cz) under your own API key. That data is then handled under the Žhavá místa terms.

The developer receives no data. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.

## Deleting data

Uploaded or unsent points can be deleted in the app menu. Uninstalling the app removes all its data from the watch.

## Contact

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/Vit882/Raysid-for-Garmin- or use the contact on zhavamista.cz.
